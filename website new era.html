What We Train

BUILT ON
EVERY ART

From beginner fundamentals to fight-team level — our coaches deliver world-class instruction across the full spectrum of combat sports, 7 days a week.

01
MMA
The complete system — striking, takedowns, clinch, and ground control built for cage competition at every level.
02
Muay Thai & Striking
Punches, elbows, knees, kicks, and pad work. Fundamentals through to advanced sparring.
03
Wrestling
Takedowns, cage wrestling, and scrambles — including dedicated fight-team wrestling sessions.
04
No-Gi Grappling
Submissions, positional control, and modern guard passing for competition-level grappling.
05
Boxing
Dedicated boxing classes covering footwork, combinations, and defence — plus sparring sessions.
